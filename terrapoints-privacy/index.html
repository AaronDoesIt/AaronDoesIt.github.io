TerraPoints — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

TerraPoints ("the app") is an Android app made by Aaron Witbeck (Aaron Does It on Google Play). It shows terrain as contour lines you can tilt in 3D, with topographic and satellite maps, place and road names, Oregon property lines, and a distance and elevation-profile tool.

The short version

The app has no server, no account, no ads and no analytics. It keeps nothing about you. The only thing it sends anywhere is a request for the part of the map you are looking at, so the map services can send back that area.

Location

Location is used only when you tap My location. If you allow it (precise or approximate), the app reads your device's location once to centre the map and mark where you are. It is used only on your device to position the map. It is never sent to us, sold, or used for advertising.

You can decline the permission and still use everything else: type coordinates into the search box or pan anywhere. TerraPoints never reads your location in the background.

What the app sends to other services

To draw the map, the app asks these services for the area on your screen. Each request names that area (map tile numbers or a bounding box). When the map is centred on you, the requested area shows roughly where you are. No name, account, device ID or advertising ID is attached.

  • Elevation and terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (Amazon S3).
  • Topographic map (Topo view): OpenTopoMap.
  • Satellite imagery (Satellite view): Esri World Imagery.
  • Place names, roads and peaks: the Overpass API, which serves OpenStreetMap data.
  • Property lines (when turned on, Oregon only): the Oregon Department of Forestry taxlot map service.

As with any request to a website, those servers can see your IP address and which area was requested. Their own privacy policies govern what they log. TerraPoints sends them nothing else.

What the app stores on your device

  • A cache of recently viewed map tiles, labels and property lines, so the map redraws without downloading again. Settings → Clear map cache deletes it.
  • Your settings and the last map view, so the app reopens where you left off.

This stays on your device. If your phone's own Android backup is turned on, your settings and last map view may be included in that backup to your Google account; they never reach us. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs, and no crash reporting that leaves your device
  • No background location
  • No selling or sharing of data for marketing
  • No tracking across other apps or websites

Data retention and deletion

TerraPoints keeps no user data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

TerraPoints is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that touches personal data, for example ads or a sign-in, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
